# Dynamax & Gigantamax Reference

**Last verified:** May 2026

## Eligibility

- **Not all species can Dynamax.** Eligibility is gated by Niantic — only Pokémon added to the Max Battle pool.
- Roster size as of May 2026: **150+ Dynamax-eligible species** across Gens 1–8
- The eligible roster expands every week via Max Mondays (new featured Pokémon) and Max Battle Day events (Gigantamax debuts)
- Trainers must be **level 13+** and complete the "To the Max!" Special Research to access their first Dynamax Pokémon

### Critical eligibility rules

- **Shadow Pokémon CANNOT Dynamax** AND cannot fill any team slot (attacker, defender, or healer) in Max Battles
- **Mega and Dynamax are mutually exclusive** — a Pokémon cannot Mega Evolve and Dynamax in the same battle
- Always verify eligibility against current Pokémon GO Hub Max Battle tier lists before recommending in Trainer Tips

## Max Particles (the Resource)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Daily soft cap | **800 MP** (resets 5:00 AM local) |
| Storage cap | **1,500 MP** (raised from 1,000 in May 2025) |
| Walking rate | **300 MP per 2 km** (claim through Power Spot tab) |
| Power Spot — first-ever interaction | **120 MP** |
| Power Spot — subsequent days | **100 MP per spot per day** |
| Research-task MP | Stacks ABOVE the daily cap |
| Code-redemption MP | Stacks ABOVE the daily cap |
| Shop bundles | 800 MP for 150 PokéCoins; 2,400 MP for 425 PokéCoins |

**Cap-bypass trick:** Visit 7 Power Spots (700 MP) + claim 300 MP from walking → 1,000 MP in one day, exceeding the 800 soft cap. Maximum legitimate per day is approximately **1,000 MP** without events or shop purchases.

## Max Battle Costs

| Tier | MP cost | XP reward |
|---|---|---|
| 1-Star | 250 MP | 5,000 XP |
| 2-Star | 400 MP | 7,500 XP |
| 3-Star | 400 MP | 7,500 XP |
| 5-Star | 800 MP | 25,000 XP |

**Particles are consumed only on victory**, not on entry/loss.

## Power Spots

- Volatile, time-limited map locations marked with a **purple X**
- Distinct from Gyms / PokéStops; located on public art, libraries, parks, etc.
- One free interaction per Power Spot per day; resets 5:00 AM local
- Power Spots drift / shift around the map after a few days
- After winning a Max Battle, players can station a Dynamax/Gigantamax Pokémon at the Power Spot. Stationed Pokémon yield Candy and Candy XL when the spot expires; more Pokémon stationed = higher chance of wild encounters around the spot
- Power Spot tier (1-star vs 3-star vs 5-star vs 6-star) determines what Max Battle is available there
- **Max Mondays:** Power Spots refresh more often and additional spots activate between **6:00 AM – 9:00 PM local**

## Group Sizes Per Tier

- **1-, 3-, 5-Star Max Battles:** Up to 4 trainers per group
- **6-Star Gigantamax:** Up to 40 trainers per lobby (some sources say up to 100, divided into teams of 4). Practical in-person minimum is ~20 trainers
- **No remote raid pass support for Gigantamax** — Gigantamax is in-person only
- **Solo / duo Gigantamax is NOT possible.** Solo Dynamax up to 3-star is possible with strong attackers; 5-star Dynamax can be soloed with optimized teams; 6-star always requires groups

## Max Phase (In-Battle Mechanic)

- Battles are real-time but switch into the **Max Phase** when the **Max Meter** fills (filled by Fast Moves faster than Charged)
- During Max Phase, the Pokémon Dynamaxes for **3 turns**, getting **3 Max Move uses** (one per turn)
- **Eternatus's Dynamax Cannon Adventure Effect** can push Max Moves to **Lvl 4** (above the normal cap of Lvl 3)
- Players CAN dodge single-target attacks by swiping; group-wide Max Moves CANNOT be dodged
- Defeated trainers can cheer to charge teammates' Max Meters

Each Pokémon's Max Moves must be unlocked and leveled separately.

| Level | Cost | Notes |

| Max Guard — Lvl 1 | 400 MP + 50 Candy | Locked by default |

## Roles in Max Battles

Niantic-recommended composition for a 4-player team: **2 Damage Dealers + 1 Healer + 1 Tank**.

| Role | Job | Best examples (May 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Attacker** | Stack Max Strike-type damage | G-Max Inteleon (Water, top), G-Max Gengar (Ghost, top), G-Max Charizard (Fire), G-Max Machamp (Fighting), G-Max Rillaboom (Grass), Dynamax Zacian (Steel), Dynamax Excadrill (Ground), Dynamax Metagross (Steel/Psychic) |
| **Defender** | Tank with Max Guard, draw aggro | Pokémon with 0.5s Fast Moves (fast Max Meter fill) + high HP/Def. S-tier favors high-HP, high-Def types with 0.5s fasts |
| **Healer** | Spam Max Spirit | **Blissey is the supreme/uncontested S-tier healer** thanks to massive HP. A-tier picks behind it. |

Always cross-check against current Pokémon GO Hub Max Battle tier lists (Attackers / Defenders / Healers) before recommending — see `instructions/meta-data-sources.md`.

## Catch Encounter After Max Battle

- **Free encounter** after winning (no Premier Balls limit)
- IV floor: 10/10/10 (typical Max Battle floor)
- Catch level: L20 standard (L25 if weather-boosted)
- **Shiny rates:**
  - Standard Max Battle: ~1/64 (raid-like)
  - Max Battle Day featured species: ~1/20 (boosted)
  - Some Gigantamax outside of events: ~1/512

## Gigantamax — Detailed System

### Differences from Dynamax

- Gigantamax is a **special Dynamax form** unique to certain species, with altered appearance + a **fixed signature G-Max Move** (independent of Fast Move type)
- Dynamax Pokémon's Max attack varies by Fast Move type — Gigantamax's G-Max Move is locked to the species
- G-Max Moves deal more damage than equivalent Dynamax Max Moves
- Encountered only in **6-Star Max Battles** (in-person, group-required)
- Cannot be transferred to Pokémon HOME

### NOT Yet in Pokémon GO

These exist in main games but haven't been released in Pokémon GO yet (May 2026): Drednaw, Coalossal, Flapple, Appletun, Sandaconda, Centiskorch, Hatterene, Copperajah, Duraludon, Orbeetle, Corviknight, Alcremie, Eevee, Melmetal, Urshifu (both forms), Eternatus (G-Max form was untradable boss-only at GO Fest 2025 Max Finale, never catchable).

### G-Max Move Effects in Pokémon GO

In GO, G-Max effects are **simplified vs. main games** — they primarily deal much higher damage. Some carry secondary effects (e.g., trapping/DoT-flavor mentioned for G-Max Wildfire and G-Max Cannonade), but the exact in-GO mechanical implementation of secondary effects is NOT Niantic-documented and varies by source.

**For Trainer Tips:** describe G-Max moves as "powerful signature attacks" rather than promising specific status/DoT effects unless verified per-Pokémon against an official source.

### Gigantamax Raid Logistics

- **6-Star Max Battle** — in-person only, 800 MP entry cost, deducted only on win
- Practical group: 20–40 trainers
- Free post-battle encounter; catch CP at L20 (L25 weather-boosted)
- Shiny rate: ~1/20 on Max Battle Days, ~1/64 baseline, ~1/512 outside events for some species

## Strategic Notes

### Investment Priorities

- **Always prioritize Gigantamax forms over their Dynamax counterparts** when available — G-Max Moves outclass standard Max Moves on the same species
- Top investment targets (May 2026): G-Max Inteleon, G-Max Gengar, G-Max Machamp, G-Max Charizard, G-Max Rillaboom, Dynamax Zacian (Crowned Sword), Dynamax Excadrill, Eternatus
- Save Candy XL for Lvl 3 unlocks on top-tier attackers — 40 XL per move is steep, requires Lv 31+ trainer

### Particle Math

- **Pure walking (no Power Spots):** 300 MP / 2 km × 1 claim ≈ 300 MP/day
- **Pure Power Spots:** 100 MP × 8 spots = 800 MP/day (hits cap)
- **Maximum legitimate per day (no events, no shop):** ~1,000 MP by combining 7 Power Spots + walking
- **Cost to run a 6-Star Gigantamax:** 800 MP per attempt → ~1 attempt per day's farming if no stockpile
- **Storage cap of 1,500 MP** = ~1 stockpiled 6-Star attempt + nearly two 3-Stars before overflow. Spend down before overflow.

### Group Composition by Tier

| Tier | Recommended group | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| 1-star | Solo possible | 1 attacker |
| 3-star | Solo with strong meta picks; safer with 2 | 2 attackers, or 1 attacker + 1 healer |
| 5-star Dynamax | 2–4 trainers (some duoable) | 2 attackers + 1 healer + 1 tank |
| 6-star Gigantamax | 20+ trainers, in-person | Multiple 4-player teams; each team should have the standard 2/1/1 split |

### Event Timing Levers

- **Max Mondays** — every Monday, 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM local Max Hour (extra Power Spots active 6 AM – 9 PM)
- **Max Battle Day** — full-day rotating Gigantamax / Dynamax featured boss; boss rotation every 30 minutes; Shiny rate boosted to ~1/20
- **No fixed "Max Hour" distinct from Max Monday** in current programming — Max Mondays are the de facto weekly Max Hour

### Replay: GO Bigger Event-Specific Bonuses (April 25, 2026 example)

These are event-specific bonuses, NOT permanent. Note for future reference:
- Daily MP cap raised to 1,600 (event window only)
- 8x MP from Power Spots
- 2x MP from walking (12 AM – 5 PM local that day)
- ¼ adventuring distance for MP (so 0.5 km instead of 2 km per claim)
- Premium Timed Research available with bonus MP

These bonuses are time-limited and shouldn't be cited as standard. Verify event-specific bonuses against current Niantic announcements.

## Source Confidence Map

**Niantic-confirmed:**
- 800 MP daily cap, 1,500 MP storage cap (post-May 2025), 4-trainer team max for 1/3/5-star, in-person-only Gigantamax, no remote raid pass for 6-star, free post-battle encounter, Lv 13+ trainer requirement, "To the Max!" Special Research, Max Phase 3-turn mechanic, Eternatus Dynamax Cannon Adventure Effect for Lvl 4 boost, Max Mondays 6 AM – 9 PM with extra Power Spots

**Community-derived but well-documented:**
- 300 MP per 2 km walking rate, 100 MP per Power Spot per day (120 first time), specific MP costs per tier (250/400/800), Lvl 1/2/3 unlock costs (400/600/1400 MP + Candy), 1/64 standard Max Battle shiny rate, 1/20 Max Battle Day rate

**Unverified — flag carefully:**
- Per-G-Max-move secondary effects in GO (DoT, trapping, status) — sources reference these but Niantic hasn't published exact mechanical specs
- Exact Lvl 4 power multiplier from Eternatus Adventure Effect — sources say "outsized" but no number is published
- 6-Star lobby cap of 100 vs 40 — sources disagree; 40 is more frequently cited and matches Niantic-published copy
- G-Max Pikachu's exact in-GO move name (labeled "G-Max Volt Crash" in main games; in-GO labeling unclear)

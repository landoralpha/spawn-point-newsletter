# Community Tips & Tricks Reference

Broader Pokémon GO community-discovered tactics and underused mechanics. Use this when writing Trainer Tips that need angles beyond the 37-angle framework, or when an event/feature aligns with one of these tactics.

This file complements `instructions/trainer-tips-framework.md` and `instructions/community-day-tips.md`.

## 1. Pokédex Search Operators

### Boolean composition
- `&` = AND, `,` = OR, `!` = NOT
- Example: `cp-1500&3*,4*&!legacy` = all 93%+ IV under 1500 CP without legacy moves
- Foundation of every advanced search string

### IV star shortcuts
- `0*` = pure trash (0 IV total)
- `1-2` = 0-35 IV total (mass transfer)
- `3*` = 82.2-97.7%
- `4*` = hundo (15/15/15)
- Combine with `!cp1500` to exclude PvP keepers

### Other useful filters
- `@special` = Pokémon with moves that can't be re-obtained via TM (Mewtwo's Shadow Ball, Purified Return, exclusive CD moves) - use BEFORE bulk-TMing
- `@1` = Quick attacks, `@2` = Charged attacks, `@3` = either
- `@2dragon` = every Dragon-type charged attacker in storage
- Standalone tags: `legendary`, `mythical`, `costume`, `traded`, `lucky`, `shiny`, `shadow`, `purified`, `defender`, `xxs`, `xxl`
- `xxs&!shiny` = every micro-form for Showcase entry
- `cp-1500` = under 1500 CP (PvP candidates)
- `cp1490-1500` = right at GL cap

### Mass evolve naming hack
- Rename evolve candidates to a single character (`a`, `1`)
- Clusters them at the top during Lucky Egg run
- Saves ~2 seconds per evolve = 8-12 extra evolves per egg

## 2. Account-Level (L41-L80)

**Trainer level cap is now 80** (raised from 50 on October 15, 2025). Levels 41-80 require XP + Level-Up Research tasks. Total XP to L80: ~203,353,000.

### Level 50 task list (gateway requirements)
- 30 Platinum medals (longest grind)
- 300 Gifts sent
- 20 Best Friends
- 999 Excellent throws
- 999 Legendary catches
- 1,000 Rocket grunts defeated
- GBL Rank 24

### Levels 51-80 (added October 2025)
- Each level requires XP + Level-Up Research tasks
- Research is immediately unlocked at each level past 70
- Rewards at L80 milestone: +200 Pokémon Storage, +100 Item Bag, +5 Gift Storage, +100 Postcard Pages

### Friend list capacity
- **Current cap: 650** (raised in stages from 200 → 300 → 400 → 450 → 550 → 650 over 2024-2026)
- Niantic help center may still show 550; the news posts and patch notes show 650
- More slots = more daily gift opens and senders for the 999-Excellent grind

### Bag capacity unlocks
- L43/53/63/73: +5 gift bag capacity at each milestone (max 40)
- Plan storage purchases around level milestones
- L80 grants additional +5 Gift Storage on top of milestones

## 3. Buddy & Best Buddy Optimization

### 21 buddies a day
- Swap buddy 20 times per day = 21 buddies receive interactions daily
- Critical for parallel Best Buddy farming

### 6-heart no-walk minimum
- Without movement: 6 hearts/day (Treat, Play, Battle x3, Snapshot)
- 300 hearts ÷ 6 = 50 days minimum to Best Buddy

### Poffin = 6 instant hearts + 2x rate for 6 hours
- Instantly fulfills daily feeding task
- Makes buddy Excited
- Doubles hearts from all other categories for up to 6 hours
- Strategy: Feed Poffin → batch all other interactions in that window

### Best Buddy timeline
- Hitting all 20 hearts daily: ~12-15 days to Best Buddy
- 300 hearts total required

## 4. Friend Gift & Postcard Mechanics

### Daily caps
- 30 gifts opened per day (hard cap)
- 1 gift sent per friend per day
- Receiving has no cap (good for L50's 300 Gifts Sent grind)

### Gift hoarding strategy
- Stockpile gifts on weekends
- Open 30/day during friendship-XP-boost events for compounded effect

### Postcard Book
- Holds 350 postcards
- Pin postcards to build region collections
- Pinned postcards from international friends = the only path to all 18 Vivillon patterns

### Stickers
- 25 cap per type, consumed on use
- Cosmetic only, but build community

## 5. Spinning, Coins, Streaks

### 7-day PokéStop streak
- = 2,500 XP + bonus items
- Day 7 spin guarantees an Evolution Item
- Memories in Motion season also gives boosted XP for the 7-day streak

### 7-day catch streak
- = 2,500 XP + 3,000 Stardust
- Plus 500 XP / 600 Stardust on first daily catch
- Hit a Lucky Egg before day 7 catch to double the streak XP

### Streak-saver mechanic
- Multiple spins/catches in same day count as one streak day
- Missing a day breaks streak
- Day rolls over at 12:00 AM local

### Gym defender 50-coin cap
- 1 coin per 10 minutes, 50 coin daily cap
- Pokémon must return defeated to claim
- Place defenders in evening so they flip overnight = full 50 coins next morning
- 8 hours 20 minutes is the threshold

### Multiple gyms ≠ stacking coins
- 6 gyms doesn't earn 300 coins
- Coordinate timing - let one return, place in next

## 6. AR Power-Up PokéStops

### Tier system
- 5 scans = Level 1
- 10 scans = Level 2
- 25 scans = Level 3
- Higher tier = more items per spin AND longer powered-up duration

### AR Mapping tasks live in their own slot
- Don't take a regular Field Research slot
- Free to clear without losing research progress
- Reward: typically Stardust + Rare Candy (flat amounts)

## 8. PokéStop Showcase Tactics

### Scoring formula
- Score = (Scaled Height × 800 / Max Height) + (Scaled Weight × 150 / Max Weight) + (IV Sum × 50 / 45) + Bonus
- Height carries the most weight
- **XXL Pokémon dominate size showcases** - a 0% XXL beats a hundo XS in size showcases

### Furfrou Trim swaps
- 25 candy + 10K dust per swap
- 9 trims total
- Each trim = a separate Showcase eligibility entry

### Vivillon = 18 patterns via Postcards
- Patterns unlocked by pinning gift postcards from specific real-world regions
- International friends are mandatory

### One Pokémon, one active Showcase
- Can't enter the same individual in two showcases
- Have multiple high-IV/XXL specimens of the same species ready

## 10. Egg & Incubator Strategy

### Super Incubator math
- 1.5x speed = 33% distance reduction
- 5 km egg hatches at 3.335 km
- Save Supers for 10 km / 12 km eggs only

### 50 km Adventure Sync 10 km egg pool (2026)
- Gible, Riolu, Goomy, Dreepy
- Frigibax has rotated through historically
- Walk 50 km Sun-Sat for the guaranteed 10 km egg

### Free Incubator only on 2 km eggs
- Wastes value if used for longer-distance eggs

### Save eggs across pool rotations
- Egg pools change with seasons
- Hold unhatched eggs from old pool - they retain old hatch list

## 11. Daily Adventure Incense (DAI)

### Galarian Birds
- DAI is the only path to Galarian Articuno/Zapdos/Moltres
- 15-min duration, walk required for spawns
- 0.3% catch rate, 90% flee
- Shiny variants (~1/20 of encounters) have 0% flee rate - keep throwing forever

### Sunday extension (Memories in Motion)
- Sunday Daily Discovery: standard Incense + Lure duration doubled to 60 min
- DAI is NOT extended

## 15. Purification Strategy

- +2 to every IV on purification (13/13/13 Shadow → 15/15/15 Purified)
- Special move "Return" replaces Frustration (Normal-type 3-bar)
- XL Candy cost: Purified < Regular < Shadow (~12% fewer XL needed)
- Purified Pokémon are tradeable; Shadow are not

## 17. Catching Timing & Multipliers

### Excellent + Curveball + same-type Mega
- Curveball Excellent throw = ~+110 XP per catch
- Stack with Lucky Egg + new Pokédex entry for ~1,000+ XP catches

### Circle Lock technique
- Hold the Poké Ball, wait for inner circle to shrink, release while holding
- Locks circle size for consistent Excellent throws

### Throw timing during attack animation
- Release ball halfway through Pokémon's attack animation
- Ball lands as circle reaches center

## 19. Niantic Wayfarer

## 21. Pre-CD Catching Behavior

### Don't catch the featured Pokémon before CD starts
- Field-encountered Pokémon at the start of CD will roll the boosted spawn pool
- Spawns waiting in the open world auto-convert
- Wait the last few minutes pre-CD to maximize encounter rate immediately at start

## 22. Hidden Interactions

### Trade stardust scaling
- Cost scales with rarity to either trader (Legendary trades = 20K-1M+ dust)
- Trade Pokémon caught nearby first to minimize dust

### Quick Catch / Try Again exit
- Tap menu → Pokémon → return → ball flies in already-thrown
- Skips encounter animation
- Works in catch streaks
- Used during DAI runs to maximize catches in 15 min

### Pinap before Mega = stacked candy
- Pinap (2x candy) + Mega same-type (+1 base / +2 L2 / +3 L3) compound
- Silver Pinap = 3x candy AND guaranteed catch

### Best Buddy +1 CP Boost
- Pokémon at Best Buddy fights as if one level higher (~+5% effective CP) when actively set as buddy
- Critical breakpoint tech for PvP

# Adventure Effects Reference

**Last verified:** May 2026

Adventure Effects are temporary world-effect bonuses triggered by using specific Charged Attacks **outside of battle**. They cost Stardust + Candy and last for fixed time blocks (extendable up to 24 hours).

## Critical Distinction

**Adventure Effects are NOT a Mega Level 3 unlock for Mega/Primal Pokémon.**

There are three separate systems that get conflated:

| System | Trigger | Who has it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adventure Effects** | Specific Charged Attack used outside battle, costs Stardust + Candy | Small specific roster of Legendary/Mythical (Origin Formes, Fusions, Crowned Forms, Necrozma forms, Eternatus) | Temporary world/encounter bonus (6 min – 24 hrs) |
| **Mega Evolution raid aura** | Pokémon actively Mega-Evolved on field | All Megas | +30% same-type / +10% other for OTHER trainers in raid |
| **Primal Reversion raid aura** | Pokémon in raid party (no activation needed beyond reverting) | Only Primal Groudon, Primal Kyogre | +30% to typed attacks for OTHER trainers in raid |

**No Mega or Primal Pokémon has its own Adventure Effect.** If you see a community claim like "Mega Garchomp's Dragon Soul Adventure Effect," it is not a Niantic-documented mechanic — flag as community speculation.

## Universal Mechanics

- **Activation:** Tap the Pokémon → use the Adventure Effect-eligible Charged Attack from the Pokémon's screen (not from battle)
- **Cost (standard):** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Candy per activation block
- **Cost (Necrozma):** 3,000 Stardust + 3 Necrozma Candy per block
- **Duration per block:** 6 minutes (Roar of Time, Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash) or 10 minutes (everything else)
- **Stacking:** Up to 2 hours can be added in a single tap-spree; total active time can extend up to **24 hours** maximum
- **Concurrency:** Only ONE Adventure Effect may be active per trainer at a time. Activating a second different effect ends the first.
- **External stacking:** Adventure Effects DO stack with Incense, Lucky Eggs, Star Pieces, active Mega Evolution, and Primal raid auras (these are separate systems)
- **Multi-player stacking:** Adventure Effects are personal, NOT shared. Each trainer activates their own.
- **Mega Level requirement:** None. Adventure Effects are tied to the move + species, not to Mega Level.
- **TM restriction:** Adventure-Effect moves typically CANNOT be obtained via Charged TM. Only learned at capture during the corresponding event.

## Complete Adventure Effects Roster (May 2026)

There are **9** confirmed Adventure Effects in the game.

### Category A — Encounter / Catch Utility

#### 1. Roar of Time — Origin Forme Dialga
- **Effect:** Pauses the timers of Incense, Daily Adventure Incense, Lucky Eggs, and Star Pieces (effects remain active; clock just stops)
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Dialga Candy per 6-minute block
- **Duration:** 6 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** Save unused Incense/Lucky Egg time during slow moments; resume during Spotlight Hour or Community Day. Highest-value Adventure Effect for casual grinders.

#### 2. Spacial Rend — Origin Forme Palkia
- **Effect:** Increased encounter distance for wild Pokémon (tap spawns further from your avatar)
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Palkia Candy per 10-minute block
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** Stationary play (driving as a passenger, sitting at one location during an event). Common during Spotlight Hour at fixed locations.

#### 3. Ice Burn — White Kyurem (Reshiram-fused with Glaciate)
- **Effect:** Catch ring slows down (easier Excellent throws); works with Pokémon GO Plus / accessory devices
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Kyurem Candy per 10-minute block
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** Pinap/Silver Pinap farming runs; Excellent throw research tasks; pair with Spotlight Hour Stardust events

#### 4. Freeze Shock — Black Kyurem (Zekrom-fused with Glaciate)
- **Effect:** Pokémon stop moving during encounters AND **+25% catch rate** (also applies to accessory-device catches)
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Kyurem Candy per 10-minute block
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** Catching jumpy/erratic species (Zubat lines, bug-types); Raid Hour for Premier Ball catches on bouncy bosses

### Category B — Spawn Attraction / Time-of-Day Evolution Bypass

#### 5. Sunsteel Strike — Dusk Mane Necrozma (Solgaleo-fused)
- **Effect:** Incense-like effect attracting daytime-themed Pokémon; enables daytime-only evolutions at any hour (Eevee → Espeon, Rockruff → Midday Lycanroc, Riolu → Lucario)
- **Cost:** 3,000 Stardust + 3 Necrozma Candy per 10-minute block
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** Need Espeon mid-night, evolve daytime forms during evening events

#### 6. Moongeist Beam — Dawn Wings Necrozma (Lunala-fused)
- **Effect:** Incense-like effect attracting nighttime-themed Pokémon; enables nighttime-only evolutions at any hour (Eevee → Umbreon, Ursaring → Ursaluna, Midnight Lycanroc)
- **Cost:** 3,000 Stardust + 3 Necrozma Candy per 10-minute block
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Best use:** **Critical for Ursaluna evolution** (otherwise locked to nighttime + full moon). Only on-demand way to evolve Umbreon during the day.

### Category C — Raid / Max Battle Stat Boosts

#### 7. Behemoth Blade — Crowned Sword Zacian
- **Effect:** +10% Attack in Raid Battles AND +5% Attack in Max Battles
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Zacian Candy per 6-minute block
- **Duration:** 6 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Released:** June 24, 2025
- **Best use:** Mega Raid Day, Elite Raid Day, back-to-back Raid Hours. Stacks separately from the Mega Evolution attack bonus, so combining a Mega lead + Behemoth Blade is currently the strongest raid DPS configuration outside Primal raid bonuses.

#### 8. Behemoth Bash — Crowned Shield Zamazenta
- **Effect:** -10% damage taken in Raid Battles AND -5% damage taken in Max Battles
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 5 Zamazenta Candy per 6-minute block
- **Duration:** 6 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Released:** June 24, 2025
- **Best use:** Low-trainer raids (duos, trios), Super Mega Raids where survival is the bottleneck

### Category D — Max Move Enhancement

#### 9. Dynamax Cannon — Eternatus
- **Effect:** Boosts every Max Move by 1 level for 10 minutes. Locked Max Moves jump to Lvl 1; Lvl 3 Max Moves become **Lvl 4** (above the normal cap). Affects all Dynamax/Gigantamax Pokémon and Eternatus itself in your party.
- **Excludes:** Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash
- **Cost:** 5,000 Stardust + 30 Eternatus Candy per 10-minute block (note: 30 Candy, higher than other Adventure Effects)
- **Duration:** 10 min, extendable to 24 hrs
- **Released:** GO Fest 2025: Max Finale (August 2025)
- **Best use:** Every Max Monday session, every Gigantamax debut day. The Lvl 4 Max Move uplift is otherwise unreachable.

## Strategic Timing Summary

| Effect | Use it on… | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Roar of Time | Spotlight Hour, Community Day, GO Fest | Pause Lucky Egg/Star Piece for double-XP windows |
| Spacial Rend | Stationary play (work, transit) | Reach distant spawns without walking |
| Ice Burn | Excellent throw research, nervous bosses | Slow ring = consistent Excellent throws |
| Freeze Shock | Raid Hour, jumpy raid bosses | +25% Premier Ball catch rate |
| Sunsteel Strike | Need Espeon at night, day evolutions on demand | Day evolutions at any hour |
| Moongeist Beam | Need Ursaluna or Umbreon | Bypass nighttime requirement on demand |
| Behemoth Blade | Mega Raid Day, Elite Raids | +10% raid damage on top of Mega aura |
| Behemoth Bash | Low-trainer raids, Super Mega Raids | -10% damage taken; survival tool |
| Dynamax Cannon | Every Max Monday, G-Max debuts | Lvl 4 Max Moves break the normal cap |

## How to Use in Newsletter Tips

When a Trainer Tip can incorporate an Adventure Effect, cite it specifically:
- ✓ "Activate Eternatus's Dynamax Cannon (5,000 Stardust + 30 Candy) before your first Cottonee Max Battle to push your Max Moves to Lvl 4."
- ✓ "If you have White Kyurem, run Ice Burn during Spotlight Hour for easier Excellent throws on the bonus catch."
- ✗ "Use a Mega Level 3 Adventure Effect" — Mega/Primal Pokémon don't have Adventure Effects.

When the featured event is a Mega Raid Day or Max Battle Day, the most relevant Adventure Effects to mention are:
- **Mega Raid Day:** Behemoth Blade (Zacian) for damage, Behemoth Bash (Zamazenta) for survival
- **Max Battle Day / Max Monday:** Dynamax Cannon (Eternatus) for the Lvl 4 boost

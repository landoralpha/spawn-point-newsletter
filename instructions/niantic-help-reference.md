# Niantic-Confirmed Mechanics Reference

**Source:** Niantic Pokémon GO Help Center + official news posts
**Compiled and verified:** May 4, 2026

## CRITICAL META RULE: Niantic Help Center Lag

**The Niantic help center FAQ pages are routinely 6+ months stale.** When a help center FAQ disagrees with an official Niantic news post (`pokemongo.com/news/...`) or a recent patch announcement, **trust the news post**. The help center should be treated as an unreliable source for any value that has been changed in the last ~6 months.

Examples surfaced during research (May 2026):
- Help center FAQ/2938 says friend cap is 550. **Actual cap is 650** (raised late 2025/early 2026).
- Help center FAQ/101 references levels 70-80. **Actual cap is 80** (raised Oct 15, 2025; was 50 before).
- Help center FAQ/4846 describes Spotlight Hour as active. **Spotlight Hours retired March 3, 2026.**
- Help center FAQ for Mega Energy says 9,999. **Actual cap is 10,000** (raised Feb 20, 2026).

When agent research finds an apparent conflict, default to the more recent authoritative source. The help center is **a starting point for mechanics, not the final word for current values.**

## Battling & Combat

### Raid Battles (faq/2187)
- Raid window: **45 minutes** to defeat the Boss after the egg hatches
- Lobby capacity: Up to 20 trainers
- Pass types: Free Daily Pass, Premium Battle Pass, Remote Raid Pass, EX Raid Pass
- Catching: Only Premier Balls work; boss flees when balls deplete
- Failure rule: Can rejoin within 45-min window without additional pass

### Raid Battle Types (faq/29)
- **Tiers:** 1, 3, 5, Mega, Mega Legendary
- **Super Mega Raids** (added Feb 2026): bosses become enraged and deploy Shields requiring Mega Pokémon Charged Attacks to break
- **Ultra Beast Raids:** Award Beast Balls (Ultra Beasts only)
- **Primal Raids:** Award Primal Energy
- **Shadow Raids:** In-person ONLY, no Remote Raid Passes
- **Elite Raids:** Egg appears 24h before boss; only **30 minutes** to challenge once hatched; in-person only
- **EX Raids: DEPRECATED.** Suspended March 14, 2020 (COVID); all EX Raid Battle data removed from the game in January 2024. Niantic help center FAQs still reference EX Raid Passes — these are stale. Old EX Raid–eligible gyms appear to have been functionally replaced by Elite Raid–eligible gyms (community-implied, not officially confirmed). Do NOT reference EX Raids as an active mechanic in newsletter content. Use "Elite Raid–eligible gym" as the modern term.

### Remote Raids (faq/2487)
- **Daily cap:** 10 remote raids per day (some events double to 20)
- **Lobby cap:** Maximum 5 remote trainers per lobby
- **Damage penalty:** Remote attackers deal reduced damage compared to in-person
- **Storage cap:** ≥3 passes = can't acquire more

### Shadow Raids (faq/4054, faq/4055)
- **In-person only** — no Remote Raid Pass
- **Purified Gem button** appears bottom-left after boss enrages
- **Purified Gems:** 4 Shadow Shards = 1 Purified Gem; max 10 in inventory; up to 5 per Shadow Raid
- **Effect stacks** when multiple trainers use them
- **Shadow Shards:** drop from Grunts, Leaders, Giovanni

### GO Battle League (faq/2312)
- Access: Trainer Level 10+
- Format rotation: Great League (1500), Ultra League (2500), Master League (no cap)
- Cannot lose rank within a season
- Rules: No duplicate species in party
- End-of-season rewards for all participants

### Trainer Battles (faq/2308)
- Great League: Max CP 1,500
- Ultra League: Max CP 2,500 (unlock = 3 Pokémon >1,500 CP)
- Master League: No CP cap (unlock = 3 Pokémon >2,500 CP)
- Reward cap: Max 3 friend/nearby battles rewarded per day

### Type Effectiveness (faq/2132)
- **STAB confirmed:** Pokémon attacks get a boost when matching their type
- **Double weakness/resistance** stacks for dual-type Pokémon
- **Immunity:** Confirmed (Niantic example: Ghost immune to Normal)
- **Specific multipliers (1.6x SE, 0.625x NVE, 0.39x doubly resistant):** NOT in Niantic articles — community-derived

### CP / HP / IVs (faq/125, faq/129)
- **Appraisal:** "Out of three stars" (not 4-star)
- **Stats appraised:** Attack, Defense, HP
- **Powering up doesn't change appraisal**; **Purifying improves appraisal**
- **Note:** "Hundo" / "4-star" is community shorthand; Niantic uses 3-star scale
- **CP formula, IV 0–15 ranges:** NOT in Niantic articles — community-derived

### Powering Up (faq/2372)
- **Candy XL:** Available at trainer level 31+
- **Sources:** Catching, transferring, hatching, evolving, trading, gym feeding, buddy walking
- **Conversion:** Regular Candy → Candy XL

### Fast/Charged Attacks (faq/1011)
- All Pokémon start with 1 Fast + 1 Charged
- **Second Charged Attack:** Stardust + Candy unlock
- **TMs:** Fast TM / Charged TM (random); Elite Fast/Charged TM (player choice, includes legacy moves)

## Catching

### Catch Mechanics (faq/102)
- **Throw bonuses:** Nice, Great, Excellent
- **Curveball:** Drag and rapidly spin Poké Ball before release
- **Berries:** Razz/Golden Razz (prevent escape), Nanab (slow movement), Pinap/Silver Pinap (2x Candy)
- **Catch ring colors:** Green = low difficulty, Orange = intermediate, Red = extreme

### Shiny Pokémon (faq/2678)
- **Sources:** Wild, Raid Bosses, Special Research Breakthroughs, task rewards
- **Restricted Shinies:** Smeargle, Unown, Meltan only via specific events
- **Specific shiny rates:** NOT stated by Niantic — see `instructions/shiny-odds-reference.md`

### Weather Boosts (faq/53)
- **Weather → Type table (Niantic-confirmed):**
  - Sunny/Clear: Grass, Fire, Ground
  - Rainy: Water, Electric, Bug
  - Partly Cloudy: Normal, Rock
  - Cloudy: Fairy, Fighting, Poison
  - Windy: Flying, Dragon, Psychic
  - Snow: Ice, Steel
  - Fog: Dark, Ghost
- **Effects:** Higher CP/spawn frequency, extra Stardust on catch, more effective moves
- **IV floor 10/10/10 and 1.2x damage boost:** NOT stated by Niantic — community-derived

## Pokémon Mechanics

### Mega Evolution
For complete details, see `instructions/mega-evolution-reference.md`.

Quick reference (Niantic-confirmed):
- 8-hour duration; one active per trainer
- Mega Energy is species-specific
- Multiple Megas in raid don't stack — highest boost applies
- **Mega Energy storage cap: 10,000** per species (raised Feb 20, 2026 from 9,999)
- 4 Mega Levels: Base / High / Max / Super Max (Super Max added Feb 20, 2026)

### Primal Reversion (faq/3774)
For complete details, see `instructions/mega-evolution-reference.md`.

Quick reference:
- Applies to Kyogre, Groudon
- 8-hour duration
- **Primal Energy cap: 9,999** per species (NOT raised to 10,000)
- Cannot power up during Primal form
- Cannot have Primal + Mega active simultaneously

### Shadow Pokémon / Purified (faq/2396)
- **Shadow:** "More damage with Fast/Charged Attacks but take more damage when attacked" (community: ±20%, not numerically stated by Niantic)
- All Shadows know **Frustration** at capture
- **Frustration removal:** Cannot via TM except during special events
- **Purification:** Improves appraisal; reduces Stardust + Candy costs; replaces Frustration with **Return**

### Lucky Pokémon (faq/38)
- **Source:** Trading
- **Aging effect:** Longer held = greater Lucky chance
- **Benefits:** Stronger battle stats, less Stardust to power up, shimmer in Pokédex
- **Specific IV floor (12/12/12), half stardust ratio:** NOT in Niantic article — community-derived

### Pokémon Fusion (faq/4736)
- **Eligible:** Necrozma (Solgaleo for Solar fusion → Dusk Mane; Lunala for Lunar fusion → Dawn Wings)
- **Indefinite duration** (vs. timed Mega/Primal)
- **Separation is free anytime**
- **Energy + Candy required** for both Pokémon involved
- **Cost does NOT decrease** with repetition (unlike Mega)
- **Improved CP**; some receive special Charge Attacks
- **Restrictions:** Cannot trade or transfer fused; cannot be Buddy
- **Multiple Fusions allowed simultaneously** (unlike Mega)
- **Shiny preserved** if base is Shiny

### Hyper Training (faq/5103) — NEW
- **Requirements:** Pokémon at Good Buddy level+; must be active buddy to progress tasks
- **Ineligible:** Shadow Pokémon, 4-star Pokémon (max IVs)
- **Bottle Caps:**
  - **Gold Bottle Cap:** Train multiple stats simultaneously, customizable
  - **Silver Bottle Cap:** Single stat, sequentially usable
  - One-time-use, time-limited (max 1 year per cap)
- **Mechanic:** 1 task = 1 stat point added (HP, Attack, or Defense)
- **Cannot transfer hyper-trained to Pokémon HOME**
- **Restored CP rises** with each stat increase

### Buddy Adventure (faq/2155, faq/2147)
- **Heart categories (8):** Walk, Treat, Play, Battle, Snapshot, Visit new locations, Follow Routes, Bonus
- **Daily reset:** Midnight local time
- **One heart per category daily** (Excited mood doubles, except Bonus)
- **Buddy Levels:** Good / Great / Ultra / Best
- **Best Buddy:** CP boost in Raids/Gyms/Rocket/PvP; ribbon
- **Poffins:** Fill hunger meter automatically; max daily hearts in "treat" category; boost mood to Excited

## Items

### Available Items (faq/3580)
- **Poké Balls:** Standard, Great, Ultra, Master (100% catch rate, "very rare"), Premier (raid/Max only), Beast (Ultra Beasts only), GO Safari Ball (event-specific, expires)
- **Berries:** Razz, Golden Razz (also fully restores gym motivation), Nanab, Pinap, Silver Pinap
- **Potions:** Restore HP "by a set amount"
- **Revives:** Half HP; Max Revives = full HP
- **Rare Candy / Rare Candy XL:** Convert to species-specific Candy
- **TMs:** Fast TM, Charged TM (random); Elite Fast TM, Elite Charged TM (player choice + legacy moves)
- **Egg Incubators:** Egg Incubator ∞ (unlimited), Standard (3 uses), Super Incubator (3 uses, faster hatch)

### Lucky Egg (faq/2483)
- **Effect:** 2x XP
- **Duration:** 30 minutes (longer during events)
- **Stacking:** Up to 200 simultaneously

### Star Piece (faq/2482)
- **Effect:** +50% Stardust
- **Duration:** 30 minutes (longer during events)
- **Stacking:** Up to 200

### Incense (faq/2480)
- **Standard:** 60 minutes (longer during events); attracts wild Pokémon
- **Daily Adventure Incense:** 15 minutes; only works while moving in real world; auto-granted at midnight local time; max 1 carried; auto-grants 30 Poké Balls if you have ≤30 when activating
- **Coin Bag Incense (rare variant):** 30 minutes; attracts Coin Chest Pokémon (Gimmighoul); boosts Mystery Coin chance from Golden PokéStop discs

### Lure Modules (faq/1789)
- **Standard:** 30 minutes (60 min on Double-Time Sunday)
- **Glacial Lure:** Water/Ice (enables Glaceon evolution)
- **Mossy Lure:** Bug/Grass/Poison (enables Leafeon evolution)
- **Magnetic Lure:** Electric/Steel/Rock (enables Magnezone, Probopass, Vikavolt evolutions)
- **Rainy Lure:** Water/Bug/Electric (enables Goodra evolution)
- **Golden Lure:** Transforms PokéStop to Golden PokéStop; attracts Coin Chest Pokémon; carry 1 at a time

### Premium Battle Pass (faq/2351)
- **Function:** Raid Battle entry OR unlock Premium Rewards in GBL
- **Sources:** Shop (PokéCoins) or Special Research rewards

### Mystery Box (faq/2789)
- **Source:** Send a Pokémon to Pokémon HOME / Let's Go
- **Effect:** Spawns Meltan in the wild
- **Cooldown:** 3 days (reduced during specific events)

### Item Bag (faq/3049)
- **Default capacity:** 350
- **Expandable:** +50 per Shop upgrade
- **Don't count toward cap:** Gifts, Stickers
- **Max Particles cap:** 1,500 (separate from item bag)
- **Current expanded caps (per Niantic news posts, Feb 22, 2026):** Pokémon Storage 11,650, Item Bag 11,000 (raised periodically; help center is stale)

### Stickers (faq/2553)
- **Cap: 25 of any single sticker design** in inventory
- **Single-use** — consumed when applied to a gift
- **Sources:** Opening Gifts, PokéStop spins, Buddy presents, defeating Rocket Leaders, Special/Timed Research, Shop, GO Plus + sleep tracking sessions

### Postcards & Gifts (faq/85)
- **Gift cap:** 20 in bag (don't take inventory space)
- **Daily cap:** 20 Gifts opened per day
- **Buddy gift drop:** Up to **10 Gifts/day** from PokéStops/Gyms
- **Postcards must be pinned BEFORE sending** the gift (cannot pin after sending)
- **Vivillon Collector Medal** (faq/2553-related): 18 patterns recognized
  - Sub-medal thresholds per region: **3 → 9 → 15** Postcards from same region for Scatterbug encounters
  - Pinning postcards from gifts unlocks regional Scatterbug spawns (counts twice daily)

## Routes (faq/4175, faq/4178, faq/4184)

- **Path types:** Niantic-created, partner-created, trainer-created
- **Length:** 0.5 km – 20 km (recommended ≤2 km)
- **Following Routes:** Trainer Level 7+
- **Creating Routes:** Trainer Level 30+
- **Start/end:** Must be PokéStops or Gyms (not event/sponsored)
- **Path:** Must be continuous
- **Following:** Tap "Follow" at the blue flag start point. Pause creates marker; must return to it to resume.
- **Manual completion:** Routes don't auto-complete — manual "Complete Route" lets you collect any missed Zygarde Cells.
- **Reward on completion:** Route Badge per Route; level up by re-completing
- **1-time star rating:** Private feedback on the Route

### Mateo (Route NPC)
- Appears at end of your **first Route of the day only**
- Once-daily Gift Exchange — random worldwide player
- **Gifts NOT taken from your bag** — generated from surrounding PokéStops/Gyms
- **Rewards:** Postcard + Poké Balls, Potions, other items
- Mateo's exchange does NOT affect Zygarde Cell spawns

### Zygarde Cells
- Collectibles found along Routes
- Routes don't auto-complete to give you the chance to collect missed cells
- Zygarde evolution requires accumulated cells (community-tracked numbers)

## GO Plus / Poké Ball Plus / GO Plus + (faq/77, faq/4161, faq/4163)

### Pokémon Sleep Integration (GO Plus + only)
- Hold main button until light flashes blue + lullaby = sleep tracking starts
- Device must be paired but NOT actively connected to Pokémon GO
- Hold again until multicolor flash to end
- Red flash = session not recorded or under 90 minutes
- **Sleep sessions must exceed 90 minutes to register**
- Rewards must be manually claimed daily
- Sleep data deleted from Niantic servers after 1 week
- Can earn stickers per sleep session

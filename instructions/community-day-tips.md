# Community Day Tips & Tricks Reference

Comprehensive optimization reference for Pokémon GO Community Days. Use this when writing the Events section for any week containing a Community Day, or for Community Day Classic / Mega Raid Day / Shadow Raid Day events that follow the same format.

## Format Quick Facts

- **Duration:** 3 hours (typically 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM local)
- **Featured move bonus window:** 4 hours after event ends (the current 2026 standard for both Regular CD and CD Classic; Niantic's FAQ still says "up to 5 hours" but is stale — always check the specific event page). See `niantic-help-reference.md` and `reference_community_day_window`.
- **Post-event raid bonus:** Often 2-hour Mega/Shadow raid window with bonus Mega Energy
- **Shiny rate:** ~1/25 during core 3 hours. Drops back to standard ~1/512 during the post-event catch buffer
- **Special Research:** Optional $1.99 ticket with 3 special-background encounters + items
- **Trade bonuses:** +1 Special Trade (typically 2 total), 50% off Stardust on all trades during event window
- **Egg bonus (2026 standard):** Eggs placed in incubators DURING the 3-hour core hatch at 1/4 distance. Place at 2:01 PM, walk later.

## 1. Bonus Stacking Math

### Star Piece on Stardust CDs
- Star Piece = +50% Stardust, lasts 30 minutes
- Bonuses stack multiplicatively
- Example chain: 100 base × 1.25 (weather) × 1.5 (Star Piece) × 2 (CD) = 375 dust per catch
- 6 Star Pieces covers the full 3-hour window
- Bonus species amplify: Staryu (750 base) × 1.25 × 1.5 × 2 = 2,812 dust per catch in rain

### Lucky Egg on XP CDs
- Lucky Egg = 2x XP, lasts 30 minutes
- On 3x XP CDs: 6x catch XP combined
- During mass-evolve with double evolve XP: 4x effect (2,000 XP per evolve, 4,000 if new dex entry)
- **Match item to bonus:** Star Piece on dust days, Lucky Egg on XP days. Don't burn Eggs on dust days.

### Friendship Level-Up Banking
- Open one gift per day to bank friendship progress
- Do NOT trigger the final Best Friend gift until inside a Lucky Egg window
- Best Friend level-up = 100,000 XP (200,000 with Lucky Egg)
- Stack multiple friend level-ups in the same Egg

## 2. Quick Catch Technique

### Mechanics
- Game registers catch when the ball contacts the Pokémon. Animation is cosmetic.
- Two-finger method: hold finger on berry/run icon (prevents menu opening), throw with second finger, release first finger when ball connects, tap back arrow before animation completes
- Cuts ~7-10 seconds per catch. Roughly doubles effective catch rate over 3 hours.
- Niantic has stated this is not bannable

### When to use vs not
- **Use:** Every CD spawn, Pinap berry catches
- **Don't use:** Shiny encounters (pause to verify + screenshot), high-CP/weather-boosted catches you want to verify, when throwing curveballs/Excellents for the bonus

## 3. Featured Move Evolution Window

- Featured move applies if you evolve during the event OR up to 4 hours after (the 2026 standard; verify per-event on the Niantic news page)
- Always check the specific event page for the exact cutoff
- All past CD moves are Elite TM-able. December CD weekend reruns ~24 previous CD Pokémon with their moves natively.
- **Strategy:** Evolve during the 3-hour core when possible (you're already mass-evolving). The post-event buffer is your safety net, not your plan.
- Set a phone alarm for 8:50 PM (or whenever the move window ends)

## 4. Mass Evolve Strategy

### Tag system prep
- Pre-tag candidates "Evolve" during catch window
- Filter by tag, evolve in batch
- Target: 60-80 evolves per 30-min Lucky Egg = 60-80K XP minimum
- With double evolve XP: 120-160K. Power players hit ~180K in one window.

### Naming hack for speed
- Rename evolve candidates to a single character (`a`, `1`) so they cluster at the top of the list
- Saves ~2 seconds per evolve = 8-12 extra evolves per Egg

### Candy budget
- Most CD evolutions: 100 candy to final form
- Stage 1 → 2 = 25 candy, Stage 2 → 3 = 100 candy
- Evolve to mid-stage during catch window, save final evolution for inside the Lucky Egg

### Trade-evolution species CDs
- Karrablast/Shelmet, Boldore, Haunter, Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler all evolve free of candy when traded
- On their CDs, batch trades with a friend = save 100 candy each
- Note: Trade-evolved Pokémon still need to be evolved inside the move window

## 5. Storage and Resource Prep

### Pokémon storage
- Clear at least 100-200 slots before CD
- 3 hours of dense spawning + mass evolves stacks fast
- Mark transfers with "Bulk Transfer" tag day-of for quick purges between Star Pieces

### Bag space
- 200+ Poké Balls minimum
- 100+ Pinaps (or Silver Pinaps - 3x candy AND catch boost)
- 6 Star Pieces, 2-3 Lucky Eggs
- 2-3 Incense (each lasts the full 3 hours during CD)

### Gift inventory
- Open gifts in advance to refill balls during CD
- Stockpile unopened gifts as ball reserves

## 6. Shiny Hunting Math

### Base rate
- CD shiny rate: ~1/25 (4%). Some 2026 CDs run at 1/20 (5%) - Niantic varies it without always announcing.
- Post-event 4-hour bonus catch window: rate drops to standard ~1/512

### Expected shinies per session
- Without quick-catch: 120-180 spawns in 3 hours solo
- With quick-catch + Incense + Lure: 250-400+ encounters realistic
- At 1/25: 10-16 shinies expected on the high end
- Variance is huge - standard deviation on 300 trials at 4% is ~3.4

### Maximize encounter count
- Quick-catch every non-shiny
- Incense in low-density areas
- Lure module at a stationary stop
- Stack with paid ticket encounters (3 extra guaranteed encounters with shiny chance)

## 7. Special Research Ticket Value

### Standard offering ($1.99)
- 3 special-background encounters with the featured Pokémon
- 5 Ultra Balls + ~24 Reward Points
- Premium Battle Pass and/or Rare Candy XL on some events
- Special background = collector value, especially on shinies

### Worth it when
- You collect shinies (3 extra rolls at 1/25 ≈ 11.5% combined chance of at least one shiny)
- The featured Pokémon is meta-relevant (PvP or Master League)
- You want the bundled Rare Candy XL for L50 grind

### Skip when
- Featured Pokémon is collection-only with a recent rerun
- You already have a hundo with the move

## 8. Mega Evolution Synergy

### Bonus catch candy
- Same-type Mega active = +1 catch candy on every same-type wild catch
- Mega Level 2 = +2, Mega Level 3 = +3
- Stacks with Pinap (+1 to the doubled pile) and Silver Pinap (+1 to the tripled pile)
- February 2026 update increased catch candy, catch XP, and Candy XL rates from same-type Mega

### Math for a Fire-type CD (Mega Charizard X active)
- Base 3 candy + Pinap = 6 candy
- + Mega same-type = 7 candy
- + 2x CD bonus = 14 candy per Pinap catch
- Silver Pinap version: 3 × 3 (silver) × 2 (CD) + Mega bonus = 19 candy

### Pre-level Megas
- Level 2 = +2 catch candy. Level 3 = +3.
- Pre-level your Megas before relevant CDs
- Dual-type Megas cover more CD types: Mega Pidgeot (Normal/Flying), Mega Beedrill (Bug/Poison)

## 9. Lucky Friend Strategy

### Mechanics
- ~1.1% chance per first-of-day Best Friend interaction
- Once Lucky Friends, your NEXT trade with that person makes both Pokémon Lucky
- Lucky Pokémon: 50% reduced power-up cost + guaranteed 12/12/12 IV floor

### Trade timing
- Hold Lucky Friend status until CD: trade two CD-shinies = both Lucky AND benefit from CD's 50% trade dust discount
- Open/send gifts daily with Best Friends to maximize Lucky Friend rolls

### Aged Pokémon trades
- Trading a Pokémon caught 2020 or earlier has ~20% Lucky chance (rises ~5% per year aged, capping at ~20% at 3+ years)
- January 2026's New Year guaranteed Lucky range expanded to include 2020 Pokémon, with cap raised from 35 to 45 trades

## 10. Friendship and Trading

### Trade allowance
- During CD: +1 Special Trade for the day (typically 2 total)
- 50% off Stardust on all trades during the event window
- Some events have run 3 specials - verify the specific event

### Trade evolution batching
- For trade-evolve species CDs: trade with friend instead of paying 100 candy
- Bring multiple to trade
- Note: Trade-evolved Pokémon still need to be evolved inside the move window

### Distance trade dust
- Bring local trade partners to avoid Best Friend distance trade penalties

## 11. Buddy Strategy

### Buddy candy farming
- Set CD Pokémon as buddy 24-48 hrs before CD to lower distance-to-candy
- Excited mood = half walking distance for candy + double hearts
- Feed before CD to maintain Excited through event

### Best Buddy CP boost
- +1 effective level CP boost when buddy is active
- 300 hearts to unlock
- Time the 300-heart push before next month's CD or PvP cup

## 12. Field Research Stacking

### Stack mechanic
- Days/weeks before CD: spin stops, complete research to encounter, then run/Razz/flee. Encounter persists in "Today" log (up to 100 stacks).
- During CD: claim encounters with Star Piece active for amplified Stardust/Candy
- Best targets: Audino (extra dust), shiny-eligible quest mons, high-base-stardust species

### Math example
- 100 stacked Audino encounters × ~1,575 dust × 1.5 (Star Piece) × 2 (CD dust bonus) = 472,500 dust in one Star Piece window

### Caveat
- Niantic has periodically nerfed or partially patched stacking - verify before relying on it for the upcoming CD

## 13. Photobombs During CD

- Daily Smeargle photobomb often replaced with featured Pokémon during CD
- Photobomb count typically increased during CD events (5-15 per day)
- Photobomb shiny rate matches CD's boosted rate (~1/25)
- **Strategy:** Take 5-10 snapshots throughout the 3 hours for free shiny rolls

## 14. Egg Incubator Bonus (2026 Standard)

- Eggs placed in incubators during the 3-hour CD: 1/4 hatch distance
- Eggs placed BEFORE 2 PM do not get the bonus
- Eggs placed at 2:01 PM get full discount even if hatched later
- **Strategy:** Clear incubators before CD, fill all 3 at 2:00 PM, prioritize 12 km / 10 km eggs for biggest savings

## 15. Incense & Lure Duration

- Incense activated during CD: 3 hours (entire core event)
- Lure Modules during CD: 1 hour each (some events extend to 9 PM for spawn attraction)
- Daily Adventure Incense: NOT extended - use before/after CD

## 16. Hundo IV Strategy

### Wild spawn floor
- Wild CD spawns: 0/0/0 floor (full 0-15 range)
- Hundo odds (no weather): 1/4,096
- Weather-boosted wild: 4/4/4 floor, 1/1,728 odds

### IV floors don't transfer between sources
- Raid floor (10/10/10 → 1/216) does NOT apply to wild CD spawns
- Weather boost (4/4/4 → 1/1,728) DOES apply to wild CD spawns
- Trade floor (1/1/1 with Good Friends, scaling to 5/5/5 at Best Friends) only on traded Pokémon

### Expected hundos
- 300 wild non-weather catches: 0.073 hundos per CD (need ~14 CDs to expect one)
- 300 weather-boosted: 0.174 hundos per CD (still <1 expected)
- Always verify weather is favorable

### Best hundo paths
- Buy the special research ticket (3 boosted-floor encounters typically have IV bumps)
- Trade with Lucky Friend (12/12/12 floor)
- Stack Stardust/Mega for the L50 power-up regardless of hundo status

## 17. Tool Recommendations

- **Calcy IV (Android)**: Live overlay reads IVs and PvP rank in <1 second per Pokémon. Best for bulk-scanning post-CD haul.
- **Poké Genie (iOS + Android)**: Screenshot-based. Only real iOS option. Also handles remote raid lobby coordination.
- **Campfire (Niantic-official)**: Coordinate local raid trains, find lured stops.
- **Niantic Wayfarer**: Sometimes overlaps with CD weekends offering bonus 3x XP at first-time stops.
- **LeekDuck / pokemongohub.net**: Best-of-class event recap with featured move data, hundo/PvP CP charts, hatch lists.

## 18. Mistakes to Avoid

- Evolving outside the move window - set an alarm for 8:50 PM local
- Forgetting to Pinap (or Silver Pinap) - every non-shiny catch should have a berry on it
- Not pre-clearing storage - storage full = forced transfer mid-rush
- Wasting Lucky Egg before evolves - evolution XP is the highest XP/min activity
- Not checking PvP rank before evolving low-IV - a 0/15/15 might be a top GL option
- Skipping the post-event raid hour - bonus Mega Energy is significantly higher than normal raid hours
- Burning Star Piece on XP day or Lucky Egg on dust day - match item to bonus
- Trading away your only hundo before checking PvP IV stat product
- Using Daily Adventure Incense during CD (it's not extended)

## 19. Date Prep Checklist

### Day before
- Clear Pokémon storage (purge to <100/total cap)
- Open all gifts to reload balls
- Pre-tag evolve candidates
- Confirm Star Piece + Lucky Egg + Incense + Pinap stockpile
- Set buddy to relevant Pokémon (or Mega Energy farming target)
- Mega-evolve a same-type Pokémon (Mega lasts 8 hrs after first evo)
- Charge phone, charge battery pack, plan route

### Morning of (before 2 PM)
- Stack Field Research encounters at nearby stops
- Send gifts to Best Friends (potential Lucky Friends roll)
- Use Daily Adventure Incense before CD
- Mega-evolve same-type

### During (2-5 PM)
- Star Piece every 30 min on dust day / Lucky Egg on XP day
- Quick-catch all non-shinies, Pinap (or Silver Pinap) every catch
- Snapshot every 20-30 minutes for photobomb chance
- Evolve in batches inside Lucky Egg windows
- Trade with friends during dust-discount window
- Place all 3 incubators with eggs at 2:01 PM (1/4 distance bonus)

### Post-event (5-9 PM)
- Claim stacked research with one final Star Piece
- Mass IV-check with Calcy/Poké Genie
- Final move-window evolves before cutoff (8:50 PM alarm)
- Hit any post-CD raid hour
- Claim Daily Adventure Incense if not used

## 20. Local Meetup Optimization

- **Lure stacking:** Cluster at multi-stop park where overlapping lures create dense spawns
- **Designate roles:** Trade hub person, raid host, lure dropper
- **Drop a defender** at local gym to passively earn coins during CD (max 50/day, 8h20m needed)
- **Coordinate via Campfire/Discord** for trade chains and raid groups

## How to Use in Newsletter

When the upcoming week contains a Community Day:
1. Note it in Week at a Glance with date/time
2. Cover in the Events section with full prose: featured Pokémon, dates, exclusive move, bonuses
3. Trainer Tip should pull from this reference - usually bonus stacking math, evolve window timing, or the most impactful tactic for THAT specific Pokémon
4. If the featured Pokémon is meta-relevant, note PvP/raid value in the prose
5. Include featured move details (PvP and PvE viability)
6. Reference the post-event raid window if applicable
7. Image at top: LeekDuck event banner

For Community Day Classic, Mega Raid Day, and Shadow Raid Day events: same format applies but with adjusted bonuses (verify the specific event page).
